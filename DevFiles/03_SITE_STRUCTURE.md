# Site Structure

## Sitemap
- `/` Home
- `/services` Full price list
- `/gallery`
- `/about`
- `/book` (contact + WhatsApp booking)

## Global
- **Header:** logo left, menu button right, "BOOK" CTA. Transparent over hero, becomes solid with blur on scroll past 80px.
- **Menu overlay:** full-screen, large serif links, staggered reveal, socials and phone at bottom.
- **Floating WhatsApp button:** bottom-right, appears after first scroll.
- **Footer:** logo, nav, socials (Instagram, Facebook "T.O.G by Tuelo" [TBC]), hours, phone, copyright.

## Home (mirrors the reference's rhythm)
1. **Hero:** full-bleed image, italic headline [draft: *"Glow you can feel."*], uppercase CTA "BOOK YOUR GLOW >" to WhatsApp.
2. **Welcome line:** one sentence intro, centred.
3. **Explore services:** headline with roman + italic split; large image cards for categories (Nails, Lashes & Brows, Makeup, Waxing, Facials, Massage, Manicure & Pedicure), each linking to `/services#category`.
4. **Brand statement:** logo mark + one sentence + "MORE ABOUT TOG >".
5. **Highlights strip:** 3 to 4 signature services with prices (e.g. Gel Extensions, Microshading, Hot Stone Massage).
6. **Gallery teaser:** 6-image grid with hover zoom.
7. **Location and hours:** Address: Molapo Crossing Mall, Gabrone. Working Hours: Weekdays: 0900 - 1800 hrs, Saturday: 0900 - 1700 hrs, Sunday: 1100 - 1700 hrs address, hours, map link, phone.
8. **Closing invitation:** [draft: "Come glow with us. *You are in the right place.*"] + CTA.
9. **Footer.**
