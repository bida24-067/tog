# Project Brief

## Client
- **Name:** Touch Of Glow (TOG) [TBC: confirm relationship to "T.O.G by Tuelo" shown on the printed price list]
- **Location:** Molapo Crossing Mall, Shop 53B, 1st Floor
- **Type:** Full-service beauty salon: nails, lashes, brows, waxing, makeup, facials, massage, pedicure/manicure

## Purpose
A **pitch prototype** to win the salon as a client for web design and booking. It should make the owner think "this is our brand, only more premium."

## Goals
1. Look luxurious and editorial, in the spirit of the Boho reference.
2. Make booking effortless: WhatsApp-first (the salon already takes bookings on WhatsApp).
3. Present the full price list beautifully and scannably.
4. Load fast on mid-range phones and mobile data (Botswana market).

## Audience
Women (and some men: "Men Buff & Shine" is listed) aged roughly 18 to 45 in the local area, browsing mostly on phones, often arriving from Instagram/Facebook.

## Primary action
"Book on WhatsApp" (prefilled message). Secondary: call, Instagram, Facebook, directions.

## Scope (v1 pitch)
- Home, Services (full price list), Gallery, About, Contact/Book
- No real payment system, no CMS, no auth
- Booking = WhatsApp deep link; optional simple request form that composes a WhatsApp message

## Out of scope for v1
Online payments, memberships, e-commerce, staff login, multi-language.

## Success criteria
- Lighthouse mobile: Performance 90+, Accessibility 95+, Best Practices 95+, SEO 95+
- Looks and feels like the reference in typography, spacing and motion
- Owner can read every price correctly
